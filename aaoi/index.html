Racing to build 800G and 1.6T capacity.

Watch list

What moves the thesis, sorted by direction.

Tailwind

Hyperscaler 1.6T win

AOI holds more than $200M of firm 1.6T orders and expected to finish qualification with a major hyperscaler, its fourth approved supplier slot.

Tailwind

Capacity ramp

800G/1.6T capacity goes from about 200,000 units a month to over 650,000 by end-2026 and 930,000 by end-2027, more than half in Texas.

Watch

Component shortages

DSP and TIA chip supply, not demand, limits 1.6T shipments; memory shortages cost $20–25M of 100G revenue in Q3.

Risk

Dilution and cash burn

Capex was $565M in Q2, funded by $539M of at-the-market share sales. More issuance is likely while the build-out continues.
