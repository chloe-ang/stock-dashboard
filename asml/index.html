ASML Holding N.V. · NASDAQ-listed shares (1 ADR = 1 share) · Veldhoven, Netherlands

The only company that makes the machines that print AI chips.

ASML is the only maker of EUV lithography machines, the tools needed to print the most advanced chips. Sales rose 21% to €9.3B (about $10.8B) last quarter, and the 2026 guide was raised to €43–45B as memory and logic makers add capacity for AI. The catches: China is still about a fifth of sales and faces tighter US export rules, and the stock trades at about 55 times trailing earnings.

Watch list

What moves the thesis, sorted by direction.

Tailwind

2027 nearly sold out

Management says 2027 low-NA EUV output is close to fully covered by orders. ASML plans about 30% more EUV (from ~65 to ~85 units) and immersion DUV capacity (from ~130) in 2027.

Tailwind

Upgrades and pricing

Installed base sales of €2.76B beat guidance by about €300M as customers paid for productivity upgrades. Service and upgrade sales should grow more than 30% this year, and management says it has more room on pricing.

Watch

No bookings number

ASML stopped reporting quarterly orders in 2026. The last figure was a record €13.2B in Q4 2025, with a €38.8B backlog, so the market now leans on what management says about 2027 and 2028.

Watch

A big Q3 step-up

The €11.0–12.0B Q3 guide is about 53% above last year's €7.5B at the midpoint, with gross margin of 55–57%. Analysts expect about $13.2B of revenue.

Risk

China and the MATCH Act

China fell to 14% of Q2 system sales but is still about 20% of full-year sales. The proposed US MATCH Act would block immersion DUV sales to China and could limit servicing there.

Risk

Valuation

At about 55 times trailing earnings the stock prices in strong growth. It fell 15% in the two weeks after the July report even though guidance was raised.
