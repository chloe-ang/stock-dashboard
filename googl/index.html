Watch list

What moves the thesis, sorted by direction.

Tailwind

Cloud acceleration

Cloud growth sped up from 48% at the end of 2025 to 63% in Q1 and 82% in Q2, and its operating income tripled. Existing customers are exceeding their commitments by more than 50%.

Tailwind

Search holds up in the AI era

Search revenue grew 17% as AI Overviews and AI Mode drew more queries. The Gemini app reached 950M monthly users.

Watch

Capex and funding

The 2026 capex guide was raised twice, to $195–205B, and 2027 will be "significantly" higher. Alphabet stopped buybacks and raised about $106B in the first half through bonds, stock and a mandatory convertible.

Watch

Paper gains swing EPS

Q2 net income of $112B included about $99B of unrealized gains, mostly on the SpaceX stake. GAAP earnings will move with those holdings.

Risk

Supply constraints

Management says Alphabet is still supply-constrained; cloud growth depends on getting chips, power and data centers online.

Risk

Antitrust

US antitrust cases over search and ad tech remain an overhang on how Google distributes search and sells ads.
