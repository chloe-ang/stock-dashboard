SK hynix Inc. · Nasdaq ADS (10 ADS = 1 Seoul share) · Icheon, South Korea

The HBM leader, now trading in New York.

SK hynix makes most of the high-bandwidth memory stacked next to NVIDIA's AI chips. Revenue rose 257% to ₩79.3 trillion (about $53B) last quarter at a 76% operating margin, and HBM4 entered mass production. The Nasdaq ADS listed in July in a record $26.5B offering and trades at a large premium to the Seoul shares, so the two prices are not interchangeable.

Watch list

What moves the thesis, sorted by direction.

Tailwind

HBM leadership

SK hynix holds roughly 60% of HBM supply and is the lead supplier to NVIDIA. HBM4 is in mass production with yields near mature HBM3E, and HBM4E samples have shipped.

Tailwind

Contracted demand

Long-term supply agreements with about 10 customers, with DRAM bit shipments guided up about 10% in Q3 and demand growth in the mid-20s for the year.

Watch

ADS premium

The ADS trades about 35% above the Seoul shares after currency conversion (₩1,681,000 ÷ 10 ≈ $124 vs $169). The premium topped 50% in July and could narrow.

Watch

One-off gains

Q2 net profit of ₩93.9T included ₩63.3T of investment gains, mainly the Kioxia stake sale, so trailing P/E flatters the business.

Risk

Memory cycle

SK hynix lost money for five straight quarters in 2022–23. Q2 2026 already missed consensus by about 5%, and HBM is sold on fixed contracts that lag spot prices.

Risk

Currency

Results are in won. The won strengthened from about ₩1,500 to ₩1,353 per dollar since June, which lifts dollar figures but trims won revenue from exports.

Calendar

Late Oct 2026

Q3 2026 earnings

DRAM bit shipments were guided up about 10% sequentially, NAND up low single digits; HBM4 ramp is the key variable.

2H 2026

HBM4 ramp

Production ramps through the second half after first mass shipments in Q2.

2027

HBM4E mass production

Samples completed in the first half of 2026; volume production targeted for 2027.
