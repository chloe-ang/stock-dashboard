Watch list

What moves the thesis, sorted by direction.

Tailwind

1.6T ramp

Record 800G transceiver shipments and the first 1.6T production shipments in Q4. Management expects 1.6T adoption to intensify through calendar 2027.

Tailwind

Optical circuit switches

OCS shipments are expanding at hyperscalers, and near-packaged optics and ultra-high-power lasers come next, with a $100M+ laser quarter targeted for Q3 FY27.

Watch

Supply is the limit

Management says supply, not demand, is the binding constraint. Capex rose to $167M in Q4 to add capacity.

Watch

Dilution from note conversions

Converting $1.1B of notes cut debt by 35% but issued new shares and created the $7.8B GAAP charge. About $2B of convertible debt remains.

Risk

Customer concentration

Growth depends on a handful of hyperscalers' AI build-outs. A pause in their orders would show up quickly.

Risk

Valuation and volatility

About 66× annualized guided earnings after a seven-fold rise. Optical stocks swing hard on AI capex headlines, so position sizing matters.
