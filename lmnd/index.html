Lemonade, Inc. · Common stock · New York

Insurance run by bots, growing faster every quarter.

Lemonade sells renters, home, pet, car and life insurance through an app, with AI handling much of the sales and claims work. In-force premium grew 32.5% to $1.43B, its 11th straight quarter of faster growth, and revenue jumped 79%. But marketing spend keeps losses going, and the stock has halved since January.

Watch list

What moves the thesis, sorted by direction.

Tailwind

Growth keeps accelerating

In-force premium growth sped up for 11 quarters in a row, and management guides 33% for Q3 and the full year. Car insurance is expanding to most US drivers by the end of 2027.

Tailwind

Better underwriting

The gross loss ratio has tightened to about 60%, and Lemonade is keeping more premium as reinsurance costs fall.

Watch

First profitable quarter

Management guides about $8M of positive adjusted EBITDA in Q4 2026 and a positive full year in 2027. Missing that would hurt credibility.

Risk

Growth spending

Marketing costs rose faster than expected in Q2 and the stock fell 24% in a day. Management says premium growth should outpace growth spend from 2027.

Watch

CFO change and Investor Day

CFO Tim Bixby moves to the board on Jan 1, 2027. An Investor Day on Nov 17 could reset long-term targets.

Risk

Catastrophes and still no profit

Storms and wildfires can swing the loss ratio quickly, and Lemonade has never had a profitable year. Analysts rate it Hold on average.
