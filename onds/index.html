Ondas Inc. · Common stock · West Palm Beach, Florida

Drones, drone-killers and robots, assembled by acquisition.

Ondas has turned from a small rail-radio company into a defense-drone group, buying a string of businesses in Israel, the US and Europe. Revenue went from $6M to $84M in a year, and it targets $525–550M for 2026. Losses are still large, deals are partly paid in stock, and the shares are down about 40% this year.

Watch list

What moves the thesis, sorted by direction.

Tailwind

Orders keep coming

Ondas has booked more than $270M of orders since June, including a $56M European order for munition safety devices on Oct 5 and US orders for its ULTRA and IonStrike drones.

Tailwind

A big cash pile

About $1.4B of cash and investments at the end of June gives room to fund losses and more acquisitions without new debt.

Watch

The H2 revenue ramp

The guide needs about $390M in H2, almost triple H1. Q3 is guided to $140–155M; shipping on time is the main test.

Risk

Dilution

Ondas pays for many deals partly in shares, including $100M of stock for GATE and Bron in September, and the share count has risen sharply.

Risk

Large losses

Q2 operating loss was $163M including $106M of non-cash charges. Company-wide adjusted EBITDA profit is not targeted until Q4 2027.

Watch

Integration

Ondas now runs many acquired businesses across several countries. Keeping them on track while scaling production is hard for a young group.
