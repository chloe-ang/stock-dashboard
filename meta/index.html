Watch list

What moves the thesis, sorted by direction.

Tailwind

The ad engine

Revenue grew 28% in Q2 and between 16% and 33% in each of the last ten quarters, as AI-driven ad targeting and ranking keep working.

Watch

Capex and costs

The 2026 guide is $130–145B of capex and $165–169B of total expenses. Q2 free cash flow was just $0.8B on Meta's own measure.

Watch

Borrowing to build

Meta issued about $55B of bonds over the last year. Total debt including leases (~$112B) now exceeds cash and securities (~$90B).

Watch

Growth slows in Q3

The $61–64B Q3 guide implies growth of about 19–25%, down from 28%, and slightly below what analysts expected.

Risk

Reality Labs

Lost $4.62B in Q2 and more than $80B since 2020. Zuckerberg says 2026 is the peak loss year.

Risk

Youth-safety trials

Several US trials over harms to young users are scheduled this year, and Meta warns they could result in a material loss.
