Teradyne, Inc. · Common stock · North Reading, Massachusetts

The test every AI chip must pass, now twice the business it was a year ago.

Teradyne makes the machines that test chips before they ship, plus Universal Robots cobots and MiR mobile robots. AI demand doubled revenue to a record $1.33B last quarter: compute test grew nearly sixfold and memory test hit a record on HBM and DRAM. The catch is lumpiness and price: Q3 guidance of $1.20–1.30B is a step down, and the stock trades at about 55 times trailing earnings.

Watch list

What moves the thesis, sorted by direction.

Tailwind

AI compute test

Compute revenue grew nearly 600% from a year ago and AI drove over 60% of sales. Teradyne qualified a second AI hyperscaler and shipped a merchant GPU order.

Tailwind

Memory and HBM

Memory test hit a record $212M, its third quarter above $200M, with orders over twice shipments. Management sees the 2026 memory test market more than 40% larger than 2025.

Watch

Q3 report and Q4 guide

The Q3 guide of $1.20–1.30B is below Q2's $1.33B on compute order timing and soft mobile. The market will look for Q4 guidance to show growth resuming.

Watch

Robotics turnaround

After a 2025 restructuring that cut about 150 jobs, Robotics grew 33% to $100M. A U.S. factory is due to open later this year, and a cobot dispute with Elite Robots was settled in October.

Risk

Lumpy orders

Test spending moves in big, uneven waves tied to a few chip makers. Revenue fell from $951M in Q3 2021 to $600M in Q1 2024 in the last downturn.

Risk

Valuation and swings

At about 55 times trailing GAAP earnings, the stock prices in more growth. It fell 34% from its Jun 30 record close to Jul 29 before rebounding.
