MP Materials Corp. · Class A · Las Vegas, Nevada

America's rare earth mine, backed by Washington.

MP owns Mountain Pass, the only scaled US rare earth mine, and is building magnet factories. A Department of War deal sets a $110/kg price floor for its NdPr and fully contracts its new 10X magnet plant. NdPr output rose 41% last quarter, but the company is still losing money while it spends $500–600M on capex this year.
