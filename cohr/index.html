Coherent Corp. · Common stock · Saxonburg, Pennsylvania

AI optics growth, paid for up front.

Coherent makes optical transceivers, indium phosphide lasers and industrial lasers. Datacenter & Communications is now 79% of revenue and grew 59% last quarter, and management targets a $3B revenue quarter by the end of FY27. To get there it is spending heavily on laser capacity, which has turned free cash flow negative.

Watch list

What moves the thesis, sorted by direction.

Tailwind

Data center demand

Data center revenue rose 66% YoY in Q4, and management expects more than 80% YoY growth this quarter. 1.6T transceivers ramp through 2027.

Tailwind

Indium phosphide scale-up

Internal InP laser output doubles YoY by the end of Q1 FY27, a quarter early, and more than doubles again by end-2027. A 6-inch wafer gives four times the output of a 3-inch one at half the cost.

Tailwind

New products

Co-packaged optics revenue starts in the December quarter, and the optical circuit switch market estimate doubled to over $4B.

Watch

Cash burn from capex

FY26 operating cash flow was only $80M against $1.1B of capex, so free cash flow was about −$1B. A third InP site is planned for the first half of 2027.

Risk

Industrial weakness

Industrial revenue fell to $431M from $511M a year earlier as Coherent leans into AI.

Risk

Execution

InP capacity is the bottleneck; any slip in yields or the ramp caps revenue. The stock is about 30% below its high, so position sizing matters.
