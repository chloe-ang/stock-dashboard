Rockwell Automation, Inc. · Common stock · Milwaukee, Wisconsin

Factory automation, pulled along by chip fabs and data centers.

Rockwell sells the controllers, drives, sensors and software that run factories, warehouses and process plants. After a two-year industrial slump, organic sales grew 10% last quarter on semiconductor, data center and warehouse projects, and the full-year outlook was raised. It is a slower, steadier story than the chip names on this site, priced at about 33 times this year's earnings.

Watch list

What moves the thesis, sorted by direction.

Tailwind

New growth markets

Semiconductor fabs, data centers and warehouse automation drove the quarter, with automotive and life sciences improving.

Tailwind

Margins expanding

Segment operating margin rose to 22.3% from 19.5%, and Software & Control earns nearly 35%.

Watch

FY27 outlook

The November report brings the first FY27 guide. The market will look for organic growth to hold near this year's 7.5–9.5%.

Watch

Recurring revenue

Organic annual recurring revenue grew 6% and software ARR high single digits, a slower but steadier part of the mix.

Risk

Industrial cycle

Rockwell's sales fell from $2.56B to $1.88B a quarter during the 2024 destocking. A turn in capex budgets would hit orders quickly.

Risk

Valuation

At about 33 times this year's adjusted EPS, the stock already prices in a solid recovery.
