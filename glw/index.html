Corning Incorporated · Common stock · Corning, New York

A 175-year-old glassmaker, rewired for AI data centers.

Corning makes specialty glass, ceramics and optical fiber. AI data centers are now the engine: Optical Communications sales rose 32% to $2.07B last quarter, and the upgraded Springboard plan targets a $20B annual sales run rate by the end of 2026 and $40B by 2030. The catch is the cost of getting there, with about $2B of capex this year and a new $2B share-sale program, and a stock that went from $255 to $124 and back to $157 since late June.

Watch list

What moves the thesis, sorted by direction.

Tailwind

AI fiber demand

Enterprise Networks sales rose 65% to $1.27B, with Gen AI products growing faster. Corning has multiyear supply deals with Meta (up to $6B), Amazon, NVIDIA and, since September, AT&T (over $3B).

Tailwind

Margins rising

Core operating margin rose 190 bp to 20.9% and Optical net income grew 77%, faster than sales.

Watch

Solar ramp

Solar (Hemlock polysilicon plus US wafers) grew 90% but lost $7M after a plant shutdown. Management expects better profit from Q3, building toward more than $3B of revenue.

Watch

Funding the build-out

Capex is set at about $2B for 2026. Corning took a $1.0B customer prepayment, sold $500M of warrants to NVIDIA and set up a $2B at-the-market share program in September.

Risk

High expectations

At about 48 times 2026 core EPS consensus, little room is left for misses. In July an in-line guide sent the stock down 12% in a day.

Risk

Concentration and legacy units

The top two Optical customers were 28% of segment sales in 2025. Management expects handheld units to fall about 15% this year on memory prices, a drag on Gorilla Glass, and Life Sciences sales fell 15%.
