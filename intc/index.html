Watch list

What moves the thesis, sorted by direction.

Tailwind

AI pulls server CPUs

DCAI revenue jumped 59% to $6.26B. Intel has signed 10 long-term contracts with server CPU buyers and says data center demand is running ahead of what it can supply.

Tailwind

18A in production

Xeon 6+ is the first server product on Intel 18A, and 18A-P has entered risk production. Non-GAAP gross margin rose from 29.7% to 41.8% in a year.

Watch

Foundry needs outside customers

Intel Foundry revenue grew 31% to $5.77B, but almost all of it comes from Intel's own products. Landing named external customers, especially for 14A, is the open question.

Watch

Capex and balance sheet

Intel is "meaningfully increasing" spending on equipment, clean rooms and substrates. Adjusted free cash flow was −$8.4B in Q2, with $50.5B of debt against $29.7B of cash and investments.

Risk

GAAP swings from the US stake

A $12.5B mark-to-market loss on escrowed CHIPS Act shares turned Q2 into an $11.0B GAAP loss. Those charges move with the share price, so GAAP earnings will stay noisy.

Risk

Supply and valuation

Substrate and memory shortages cap shipments. At ~70× annualized guided EPS the stock is priced for the recovery, and it fell 42% in five weeks after the June peak.
