Watch list

What moves the thesis, sorted by direction.

Tailwind

Robotaxi and FSD

Unsupervised robotaxi runs in 7 US metros and Cybercab production has started in Texas. Paid FSD users reached 1.48M, with take rate above 55% of North American deliveries.

Tailwind

Volume is back

Q3 deliveries of 486,532 beat consensus by about 25k after Q2 rose 25% YoY. Storage deployments hit a record 13.7 GWh, with Megapack 3 due in 2026.

Watch

Margin squeeze

Auto gross margin ex-credits fell to 16.3% from 19.2% in Q1. Q2 GAAP profit leaned on a ~$1.0B unrealized SpaceX gain; operating income was only $398M.

Watch

Capex and cash burn

2026 capex is guided above $25B. Free cash flow was −$1.09B in Q2 and management expects it to stay negative through 2026. Cash and investments stand at $43.5B.

Risk

Credits and execution

Regulatory credit revenue fell 67% YoY to $146M. Optimus, Semi and Cybercab all have to ramp in the same half-year, and the robotaxi city target for H1 was missed.

Risk

Valuation and dispersion

About 164× 2027 earnings. Analyst targets run from $157 to $840, and 15 of 82 rate it a sell. Average daily range is about $11, so position sizing matters.
