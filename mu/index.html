Memory is the bottleneck, and Micron collects the toll.

Watch list

What moves the thesis, sorted by direction.

Tailwind

Supply stays tight

Management expects memory supply and demand to be much tighter in 2027 and 2028 than in 2026, with no line of sight to balance. 26 strategic customer agreements cover about 35% of output through 2030.

Tailwind

HBM pricing reset

HBM prices for calendar 2027 were raised significantly to close the profit gap with conventional DRAM. Over 75% of FY27 shipments are already committed.

Watch

Capex step-up

About $25B of capex in the first half of FY27, including ~$11.5B in Q1, mostly cleanrooms that produce from late 2028. Cost per bit is guided up by double digits in Q1.

Watch

Capital returns

Micron plans to return 100% of excess cash, mostly through buybacks, with larger returns starting Dec 9, 2026. It holds $73.5B of cash and investments.

Risk

The cycle turns

Memory has always swung from shortage to glut. The 86.25% gross margin guided for Q1 is the first guide in this cycle below the prior quarter's actual.

Risk

Volatility

The stock is up more than five-fold in a year with a beta above 2. Daily swings are large, so position sizing matters.
