Bloom Energy Corporation · Class A · San Jose, California

Power for AI, delivered on site.

Bloom's solid oxide fuel cells let data centers make their own power instead of waiting years for a grid connection. Revenue hit $1.07B last quarter, up 166%, and 2026 guidance was raised twice to $3.9–4.2B. Oracle, AEP and all major US hyperscalers are customers or have validated the product.
