Watch list

What moves the thesis, sorted by direction.

Tailwind

Datacenter as a growth pillar

Datacenter revenue doubled quarter on quarter to $2.98B, a third of sales, up from $213M a year earlier. FY26 datacenter revenue rose 437%.

Tailwind

Pricing power

About two-thirds of Q4's sequential growth came from higher prices. Q1 FY27 is guided to another record, $10.3–10.8B at 83–85% gross margin.

Watch

Cash returns

Debt is fully repaid and cash is $4.8B. Sandisk bought back $4.5B of stock in Q4 and has $15.5B of authorization left after a new $14B approval.

Watch

Customer prepayments

Ten prepaid supply (NBM) agreements have been signed since April. They lift reported free cash flow: FY26 was $11.5B reported versus $8.7B adjusted.

Risk

NAND is cyclical

Margins went from 26% to 85% in a year on price. When supply catches up, prices and margins can fall just as fast. Consumer revenue already fell 32% last quarter.

Risk

Volatility

Beta is above 5 and the average daily range is about $96, roughly 6%. The stock fell 56% in five weeks after the June peak, so position sizing matters.
