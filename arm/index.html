Watch list

What moves the thesis, sorted by direction.

Tailwind

Data center royalties

Data center royalties more than doubled YoY for a third straight quarter as hyperscalers ship custom Arm CPUs.

Tailwind

Higher royalty rates per chip

Armv9 and Compute Subsystems carry higher rates. Over 70% of forecast royalties through FY31 are already under contract.

Watch

Royalty growth slowing

FY27 royalty growth outlook cut from ~20% to high teens; Q2 guided to low teens on a double-digit smartphone decline.

Watch

SoftBank concentration

The SoftBank licensing and design-services deal was $193M of Q1 licensing, with ~$200M a quarter expected. Related parties were ~30% of revenue.

Risk

FTC probe

The stock fell after the July 29 report on news of a US FTC investigation, despite beating estimates.

Risk

Valuation and volatility

Near 90× forward earnings with a beta of ~3.8. Average daily range is about $19, so position sizing matters.
