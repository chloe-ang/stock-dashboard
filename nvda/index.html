Watch list

What moves the thesis, sorted by direction.

Tailwind

Demand outruns supply

Q3 is guided to $108B, and Vera Rubin should be about 20% of data center revenue in Q3, with hyperscale growth re-accelerating in Q4.

Watch

Memory costs hit margins

Management says memory prices rose more than expected. Gross margin is guided to 74% in Q3 and 71–72% in Q4 before price increases restore 72–73% in FY28.

Watch

Longer payment terms

Days sales outstanding rose to 60 as Nvidia gave big customers longer to pay, and inventory reached $32B ahead of Vera Rubin. Free cash flow fell to $21.4B from $48.6B.

Risk

China and concentration

Guidance assumes no China data center compute revenue. A handful of hyperscalers and AI labs buy most of the output.
